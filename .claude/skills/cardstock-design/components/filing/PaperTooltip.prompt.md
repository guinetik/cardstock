Use `PaperTooltip` for any hover explanation. It is a note clipped to the sheet — tilted 0.65°, stock background, contact shadow — not a dark inverted bubble.

```jsx
<PaperTooltip lead="Waiting on Finance" meta="5 days · SLA 5d" hint="Past its SLA — the badge turns red." />
```

The paperclip glyph is part of the component; do not draw another. Keep the note to three short lines.
