# #24 — Watch a card and get notified when it changes

## Ask

Hap, on the [[2026-09-08]] call, looking for a way to flag priority on a card without chasing it verbally:

> Hap: *"Is there… is there any way I can click on something, and you guys get notified that… you know, like… if I said express interest. Or, like, Hap's priority."*

Joao named the precedent and committed to it live:

> Joao: *"Fuck, that's such a good idea. Other systems, like Jira and Trello, they had something called Watch. Which means that you are watching this issue. And usually people get notified like, hey, Hap is watching this issue."*

> Joao: *"So, I will make a point to add a watch feature like Jira, so you can do that. And we didn't actually have the ability to send emails until, I think, Sunday, but now we do, so I'll make sure to send an email for when you watch, put things to watch. So there will be a little eye icon, like an eye, to indicate that you are watching that. And maybe we can have a page of only your watched stuff."*

> Hap: *"Yeah, exactly. Yeah, the stuff that I'm watching, right?"*
