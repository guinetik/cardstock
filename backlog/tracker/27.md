# #27 — A board picker by the user's avatar

## Ask

Hap, on the [[2026-09-08]] call, describing where he wants to land and how he wants to switch context:

> Hap: *"I can decide what thing I want to focus on here. Is it the designer? Is it the website? Is it delivery? And I could say, I just want to spend a couple of minutes on the delivery, and I would just want the dashboard."*

> Hap: *"I need one place where, just like when you're driving in a car, you don't look at your dashboard [for long]… A dashboard is designed to be simple, with the needles, so that you can just look at it for half a second, and you know exactly what you're looking at."*

Scoped down after discussion: a board picker living by the user's avatar, not a separate landing page.
