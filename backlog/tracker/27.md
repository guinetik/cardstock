# #27 — A board picker by the logo

## Ask

Hap, on the \[\[2026-09-08]] call, describing where he wants to land and how he wants to switch context:

> Hap: *"I can decide what thing I want to focus on here. Is it the designer? Is it the website? Is it delivery? And I could say, I just want to spend a couple of minutes on the delivery, and I would just want the dashboard."*

> Hap: *"I need one place where, just like when you're driving in a car, you don't look at your dashboard \[for long]… A dashboard is designed to be simple, with the needles, so that you can just look at it for half a second, and you know exactly what you're looking at."*

Scoped down after discussion: a board picker living by the page logo, not a separate landing page.

## Status

**Done — reviewed and ready for deployment.**

A picker beside the cardstock wordmark lists accessible boards by project and
marks the current board. It works from account pages and nested board views,
with keyboard navigation and an empty state for members without boards.

The picker uses a manila filing tab with a dark spine, crisp offset shadow, and
a ruled board index. Light and dark themes and the narrow-screen layout have
been checked. All three browser tests, TypeScript, and changed-file lint pass.
