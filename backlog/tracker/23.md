# #23 — A card's title cannot be edited after it is created

## Ask

Sanjay, on the [[2026-09-08]] call, raised alongside the clone-card request:

> Sanjay: *"The second thing is I cannot edit the title of the card once, if I make… or how do I do that, if you know already how to do that."*

> Sanjay: *"Because sometimes I make a typo, and then there's no way to go back."*

## Status

**Done — reviewed and ready for deployment.**

Card titles can now be corrected from the card page or board dialog. Changes save
on blur or Enter, trim surrounding spaces, and appear in the heading and board.
Empty titles and titles over 240 characters are rejected with a visible error.

All three browser regression tests, TypeScript, package checks, and changed-file
lint pass. The full lint command remains blocked by existing issues in
`.claude/skills/cardstock-design`.
