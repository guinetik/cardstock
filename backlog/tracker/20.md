## Status

Shipped and accepted. `/` is the public landing page, built from the Claude Design concept in `tmp/invite-only homepage design.zip`.

**What changed.** The binder moved to `/projects` and `/login` stays as its own route. The topbar left the root layout for a new `(app)` route group so the landing carries no app chrome; parentheses, so no URL changed. `proxy.ts` opens `/` by exact match rather than by prefix, since `PUBLIC`'s `startsWith` would otherwise have opened the whole app. The page carries the sign-in rail, the hero, a board mid review, five numbered sections, the "not built yet" list and two invite forms.

**Mail.** cardstock owns no provider. `src/lib/mail.ts` posts to the guinetik backend's managed `/email/send`, which already holds the provider config and the throttle. `requestInvite` validates, escapes and mails `OWNER_EMAIL`. No table and no row: the request goes where the decision gets made.

**Demo content** on the page is a static fixture rendered through the real paper classes, so nothing on a live board is exposed to an anonymous reader.

**Commits.** `9b629b5` (the work), `cd93a5f` (claim), `f96db2b` (this close-out).

**Verified.** 431 unit tests, `tsc` clean, `e2e/homepage.spec.ts` 4/4. Checked in a browser at 1600px in both themes and at 430px, where the rail drops its `min-h-screen` so a phone does not open on a full screen of password box. Deployed to production, and an invite request sent from the live form arrived in the owner's inbox with the address, the team and the note all intact.

**Residuals.** `GUINETIK_MAIL_KEY` is now the one environment variable this page depends on; without it both forms refuse politely instead of pretending. Copy follows the design system's voice with two rules from the ask on top: no em dashes and no "not X, but Y".
