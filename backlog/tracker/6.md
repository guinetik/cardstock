# #6 — The board is hard to find from the project page

## Ask

On the [[2026-09-01]] call, asked to look at a lane, Hap could not get to the board:

> Joao: *"Yeah, if you go to the board, there is a lane called Leads Hap."*

> Hap: *"Where's the board?"*

> Joao: *"So go back to the — there's a link above. Yeah, right there."*

Joao hit the same seam later while driving himself, distinguishing the project page's board preview from the board:

> Joao: *"We have the board, right? We have a little representation of our board and stuff. But that's not even what I want to go. I want to go to the board itself."*

## Status

**Backlog — not started.**

## Rationale

The project page carries a small representation of the board, which reads as the board rather than as a link to it. Someone who has been sent to "the board" stops there, because the thing in front of them looks like the thing they were sent to see.

Two people hit this in one call, one of them the person who built it. That makes it a layout problem rather than an onboarding one.
