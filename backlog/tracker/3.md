## Rationale

This is the cheap half of [[1|#1]]. Hap's calendar ask is partly satisfied by something already built; he simply never found it, because it is not on the screen he opens. Putting the existing chart where he looks buys most of the value before any calendar work starts, and will sharpen what the calendar actually needs to add.

"I didn't see it was there" is worth reading as a discoverability finding in its own right, not only as a missing placement — see [[6|#6]] and [[7|#7]] for the other two instances of it in the same call.
