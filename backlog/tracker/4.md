# #4 — Charts come up empty because most cards have no dates

## Ask

Demonstrating the epic chart to Hap on the [[2026-09-01]] call, Joao hit the data problem live:

> Joao: *"Yeah, we kind of have that. So if we come here, the problem is data, right? The Epic one, if you come, for example, website and marketplace, there is supposed to be, but it's here, 18 tasks need complete dates. So we need to, someone needs to come."*

And a moment later, on a second epic:

> Joao: *"oh, one completed epic, there is no test on composition IDC."*

## Status

**Backlog — not started.**

## Rationale

Every dated view — the existing chart, and the calendar Hap asked for in [[1|#1]] — is only as good as the dates on the cards, and most cards do not have them. Shipping a calendar onto this data would show Hap an empty screen and cost the tool its credibility with him on the first look.

The board already knows which cards are missing dates; it counted them out loud in the demo. Turning that count into a prompt — which epic, how many cards, filled in from the card face — is the work that makes the chart worth drawing. It is a precondition for #1, not a follow-up to it.

- `delivery/designer/feedback/2026-09-01.transcript.txt`, 19:46.
- The same gap on the Staffeto side: of 117 designer tracker items, 46 carry a `target` and 61 a `shipped` date.
