# #10 — Board Lane Rework — lanes cannot be rearranged, and some cannot be touched at all

## Ask

Two pieces of feedback from a board walkthrough on 2026-09-03, both from someone reading the lanes for the first time.

**The order should be chronological.** Having worked out what the three work lanes meant, the reviewer described them as a sequence and expected the layout to match:

> *"now means this is stuff that you're working on today."* — *"Next means as soon as I'm done with this, everything in the next call over is what you're gonna work on after that."* — *"And then after that, everything in the later is after that."*

> *"things basically move from later to next to now."* — *"that tell you that this is basically left to right, you know, like most normal progression, like a Gantt chart."*

**Two lanes were not stages at all.** Asked what they meant, neither answer described a position in the flow:

> Reviewer: *"What does park mean?"* — *"It's like… it's not really un-nice to have. It's like, let's not touch on this right now."* — Reviewer: *"Can we just put that in unsorted?"*

> Reviewer: *"What does process mean?"* — *"it's not a specific task, it's something that we can do to improve our process."*

> Reviewer: *"could we just have tags that distinguish what is a process task versus what is a development task?"*

**Then the board owner tried to do it.** Lanes move one step at a time with no drag, and the lane most in need of retiring turned out to be one of the frozen ones.

## Status

**Backlog — not started.**

## Rationale

Three separate faults, and the third is the one that makes this a defect rather than a preference.

**Reordering is one nudge at a time.** `moveLane` takes a `delta` of `-1 | 1`, so moving a lane across five positions is five round trips, each re-fetching the lane list. There is no drag, and no way to state a target position.

**A lane's kind is decided at birth and never again.** `LanePatch` carries only `name` and `color`; nothing in the app can change `kind`. So whatever a lane was seeded as, it stays.

**Anything that is not a `work` lane is frozen in all three directions.** `move_work_lane` raises *"Only work lanes can be moved"*, `delete_work_lane` raises *"Only work lanes can be removed"*, and `updateLane` refuses with *"Only work lanes can be renamed"*. Together with the point above, a lane seeded as `inbox`, `waiting`, `built`, `done` or `archive` can never be moved, renamed or deleted by anyone, through any screen. It is permanent.

That is how a board ends up with a lane named after one particular person that nobody can rename or remove. The guard was written to protect the ends of the workflow — the seed notes archive is "intentionally the hard right edge" — but it was applied to every non-work kind, and `waiting` is not an edge. It is an ordinary lane that happens to carry an SLA.

The template question sits on top of this. A category lane is a one-way door: a card can only be in one lane, so filing it under Parked or Process costs the ability to say where it is in the flow. A tag costs nothing — a card can be a process task *and* be next. A default board should ship stages only.

## Residuals

- The narrow fix is to let `waiting` move and be renamed, keeping the guard for the true edges. The broader fix is to make `kind` patchable and let position rules stand on their own. The second is better and larger.
- Left-to-right progression and "most urgent first" are opposite orders and both are common. Whichever the default takes, the other reading must still make sense, or ordering wants to be a board setting rather than a fixed template.
- Retiring a lane on an existing board is a migration, not a template edit: its cards need a destination and a tag. `deleteLane` already takes a destination lane, so the mechanism exists once the lane is unfrozen.
- The tag vocabulary that would replace the category lanes is not designed.
