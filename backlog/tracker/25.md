# #25 — Filter cards by overdue or forgotten

## Ask

Hap, on the [[2026-09-08]] call, pointing at the board's existing overdue coloring:

> Hap: *"I don't think so, but that would be nice, because that way I could just say, hey, show me all the P1s that are… And I'd say, guys, real quick, let's just spend 2 minutes, get everything that's overdue P1 done."*

> Joao: *"Oh, you cannot filter by them? Yeah, I'll put that in as well."*

Repeated later the same call, pointing at the orange overdue markers directly:

> Hap: *"You see this? You see the overdue, right? The oranges? If you could put a filter here that that allows me to take a look at… anything that's overdue or severely overdue."*

> Joao: *"Yep, we talked about it. Overdue and the forgotten are getting in as new filters. By tomorrow, I'll have them."*

## Status

**Done.** Shipped same-day in `be6d712` ("Add board smart tag filters beside priority") — `Overdue` and `Forgotten` smart-tag filter entries sit beside Priority in the filter bar (`src/components/board/filter-bar.tsx`), backed by `src/lib/filters.ts`.

## Rationale

The board already computes and displays "overdue" (gate-4 not reached by target date, orange) and "forgotten" (no activity in a while) as visual states — this is a filter gap on existing data, the same shape as [[9]]'s priority filter.
