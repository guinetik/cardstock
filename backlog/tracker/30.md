# #30 — Stamp new cards from a stencil instead of retyping the checklist

## Ask

From the 2026-09-09 call. Hap, on what a delivery card contains: "On the subtasks, I believe for delivery, it's always going to be the same five steps. If we find that there's a sixth, we can add a sixth." And the shape of Sanjay's week: every new client means one card per integration, each given the same checklist by hand — credentialing, field mapping, development, testing, sign-off, go-live. The list never changes; he retypes it once per integration, several integrations per client.

The checklist is a property of the kind of work, not of the card. Nothing in the product says so, so it gets re-entered every time — and two cards at 3/5 mean nothing comparable until the lists are the same list.

## Rationale

Stencils are what turn the checklist from a per-card note into data worth aggregating: when every delivery card is stamped from one stencil, 3/5 means the same pipeline position everywhere and can roll up per client. Packets (a set of stencils stamped together) are phase 2 and out of scope here.
