# #2 — Cards do not show how old they are

## Ask

The second of Hap's two closing asks on the [[2026-09-01]] call:

> Hap: *"And so, on that, what we should have is, we should have something that says the date, and I'm pretty sure you were probably already flagging the date that that card was created, right? And we should just have the age of that, like, a running number that says days old."*

He gave the reason immediately, and it is the actual requirement:

> Hap: *"if we said, what's the oldest one? And we could just say, let's get rid of those ones first, so that we're always kind of, you know, our average age of P1s is low, and that way we kind of know if we've forgotten something."*

> Hap: *"if we have things that, you know, we were excited about, or we had to have 4 months ago, and we just lost track of that because we're celebrating all the cool stuff now, I don't want those things to get lost, so that's what I'm looking for, a way to track those."*

## Status

**Backlog — not started.**

## Rationale

The data is already there: cards carry a raised date, and Joao said so on the call — *"Yeah, we do have, um… Raised."* What is missing is the arithmetic and the display.

Hap asked for it "on the top page" too, which points at the age belonging in the planning roll-up as well as on the card face — an average age of open P1s is the number that tells him whether anything has been forgotten, and that is a roll-up, not a per-card badge.
