# #1 — A calendar view showing how each initiative progressed over time

## Ask

Hap raised it early in the [[2026-09-01]] call, unprompted, on first seeing the board:

> Hap: *"You know, real quick, Joao, if there was, it'd be cool to have a calendar view here."*

> Joao: *"Yeah, we have something similar as a timeline, but I will invest in a calendar."*

He came back to it an hour later as the first of two closing asks, and this time described the shape he wanted:

> Hap: *"Two things that I would like to ask, if you could put them on, that would really take this thing to, you know, kind of like the right perspective for me… One is that calendar view, right?"*

> Hap: *"The website objective initiative is one initiative. The self-service UI is another initiative. You know, uh, deployments is an ongoing initiative, right? But if I click on any one of them… it should show, okay, this is what this initiative started. Right? Within the epic, there's stories, and this is when the stories started."*

> Hap: *"And, I mean, it could be as simple as bars going from left to right with initiative names or, you know, story names, and the overall board. You know, kind of like a Gantt chart."*

> Joao: *"I'll get you a calendar, okay?"*

## Status

**Backlog — not started.**

## Rationale

Hap uses *initiative*, *epic* and *board* interchangeably and said so himself — *"I don't know the right term… I'm using them interchangeably here, maybe I'm using them wrong"* — so the view should work off whatever grouping the board already has rather than introduce a new one. Epics are the closest existing match.

A timeline already exists and is not this. The timeline answers *what arrived when and what needs attention*; a calendar answers *how long did this take and what overlapped*. Joao drew that distinction on the call and still agreed to build the calendar, so the two are meant to coexist, not replace one another.
