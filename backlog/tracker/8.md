# #8 — "Take stock" does not say what the page is

## Ask

On the [[2026-09-01]] call, part-way through the tour:

> Hap: *"All right. By the way, before I just forget, what is what is take stock?"*

> Joao: *"It's the dashboard. It's for you to take stock of your cards in card stock. So, see, it takes stock of it, puts in, uh, the cards in the epic."*

Sanjay pushed back on treating it as a problem:

> Sanjay: *"But take stock, Hap, is a very common phrase. So I this time I would not say I didn't know about it. I know about it."*

> Hap: *"Yeah, no, it kind of makes sense to me."*

## Status

**Backlog — not started.** Filed as a question, not a defect.

## Rationale

Hap accepted the name once it was explained, which is the weakest possible evidence for changing it — and the fact that it needed explaining at all is the argument for a subtitle rather than a rename. The pun ties the page to the product name and is worth something.

The decision worth making is not the word but whether any nav label in the app has to stand on its own for a first-time visitor. Answer that once and this item resolves itself along with the next three like it.
