# #7 — The lane that needs you is off-screen behind a horizontal scroll

## Ask

The [[2026-09-01]] call, immediately after Hap asked how to see what was waiting on him:

> Hap: *"So, how do I, A, look at all the things that need Hap, right?"*

Getting him there took six turns of directions:

> Joao: *"So if you scroll to the left or to the right… There should be a scroll bar below. Yep… All the way, yep, needs a path, right there. So, no, no, no, you scroll too much… Yeah, right there. Needs help."*

Joao offered the fix in the moment:

> Joao: *"So happy. And if you want, we can put this one right at the left side so you don't have to scroll."*

## Status

**Backlog — not started.**

## Rationale

A waiting lane exists to prompt a particular person, so its value is a function of how easily that person can see it. Sitting eighth from the left behind a horizontal scroll bar inverts that: the lane that most needs attention is the one that takes the most work to reach.

Joao's in-call offer — move it to the left — is one answer, but a per-viewer answer is better than a global reorder: the lane that matters is different for each person looking at the board. Worth deciding which before building, since the global move is minutes of work and the per-viewer one is not.
